# Barangay League Management System

## 1. Introduction

The Barangay League Management System is a web-based sports tournament management platform designed to digitize and simplify the administration of local community leagues.

The system provides organizers with tools to manage tournaments, teams, players, schedules, game results, statistics, standings, and playoff brackets.

Although the initial implementation focuses on basketball and volleyball tournaments commonly conducted by barangays, the system is designed to support additional sports in future releases.

---

## 2. Problem Statement

Many barangay and community sports leagues still rely on manual and paper-based processes for tournament administration.

Common challenges include:

* Manual recording of player statistics
* Difficulty tracking historical records
* Inaccurate standings computation
* Loss or damage of paper records
* Time-consuming schedule and bracket management
* Limited accessibility of league information to players and spectators

These issues reduce operational efficiency and increase the likelihood of human errors.

---

## 3. Project Goals

The project aims to:

* Digitize tournament management processes.
* Simplify league administration.
* Reduce manual encoding errors.
* Automate standings computation.
* Maintain historical records of teams, players, seasons, and games.
* Provide accessible league information to organizers, players, and spectators.
* Support multiple sports through an extensible architecture.

---

## 4. Project Scope (Version 1)

Version 1 shall include the following modules:

### Tournament Management

* Create and manage seasons
* Manage sports
* Manage divisions

### Team Management

* Register teams
* Manage team participation per season
* Manage team rosters

### Player Management

* Register players
* Track player participation history
* Assign players to seasonal rosters

### Game Management

* Schedule games
* Record game results
* Encode player statistics
* Finalize games

### Statistics and Reports

* Compute player statistics
* Compute team standings
* Generate league reports

### Playoff Management

* Generate playoff brackets
* Record playoff game results
* Track tournament progression

### User Management

* Authentication and authorization
* Role-based access control

---

## 5. Stakeholders

The primary stakeholders are:

* League Administrators
* Tournament Organizers
* Committee Members
* Encoders and Statisticians
* Coaches
* Players
* Spectators

---

## 6. Target Users

The system supports the following user groups:

### Administrative Users

Responsible for league setup, management, scheduling, and monitoring.

### Encoders

Responsible for encoding game results and player statistics.

### Viewers

Players, coaches, and spectators who require read-only access to league information.

---

## 7. Expected Benefits

The system is expected to provide the following benefits:

* Faster tournament administration
* Reduced manual errors
* Automated standings computation
* Improved record retention
* Better transparency and accessibility
* Historical tracking of player and team performance
* Increased engagement among players and spectators

---

## 8. Access Platforms

### Web Application

The web application shall provide all administrative and data entry functionalities.

### Mobile Access

Viewers shall be able to access schedules, standings, statistics, and game results through mobile devices in future versions.

---

## 9. Out of Scope (Version 1)

The following features are excluded from Version 1:

* Live game streaming
* Real-time score updates
* Push notifications
* Team self-registration
* Online payment processing
* Mobile application development
* Multi-language support
* Advanced analytics and AI predictions

These features may be considered in future releases.
